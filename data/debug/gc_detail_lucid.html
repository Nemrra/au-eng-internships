    About Lucid

    Lucid, a leader in professional services since 2000, is committed to redefining engineering and asset advisory in Australia. Our ambition is to be "Australia’s most forward thinking, agile, and influential engineers and asset advisors". With a robust team of over 350 professionals across Australia, our trajectory aligns with a vision of national growth and a commitment to "creating a better world".

    Lucid's culture centres on a people-first approach, where wellbeing and development are a primary focus for business owners and leaders. We foster an environment of openness and collaboration, encouraging the sharing of insights and innovation. This approach is not just our strategy; it's the essence of why we exist, driving our mission, and embedding our values in everything we do.

     

    About the Role

    We currently have an available position for an Undergraduate Electrical Engineer within our Adelaide Office.

    • Engineering design and calculations.
    • Understanding and application of Engineering modelling software (including AutoCAD & REVIT)
    • Reporting and written communication.
    • Client / contractor / vendor interface.
    • Attendance at industry events as a Lucid representative.
    • Attending project / site meetings with Project Team Leaders.

     

    About You

    To be successful in this role it is expected that you will have the following:

    • Be studying an Australian-recognised tertiary degree in engineering or equivalent.
    • Excellent problem-solving skills.
    • Excellent communication and people skills.
    • A passion for sustainability, a progressive mindset, and a desire to challenge current practices.
